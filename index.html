How to use TypeTrainer

A quick guide to everything in the app — no jargon, just what each part does and when to use it.

⌨ Start typing

Open the Practice tab, click on the text, and type. The timer starts with your first key press. Correct letters light up, mistakes turn red, and letters you go back and fix show in green. When the time is up, your speed (WPM) and accuracy appear. Press Enter for a new test or Esc to stop early.

⏱ Test

A normal typing test. Pick how long you want it to be (15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds) and type. Use this to measure your speed and set personal bests. The text quietly includes the letters and words you struggle with, so every test is also practice.

🎯 Sprint 90s

A short, focused drill on one weak pattern — for example a letter pair that always slows you down. Over 90 seconds it moves through four steps: first the pattern on its own, then in small chunks, then in words, then in natural sentences. Great when you want to fix one specific problem fast.

🔄 Daily loop

Your daily workout, split into one-minute parts: an easy warm-up, targeted work on your weak patterns, natural text, and a cool-down. After each part the clock pauses at a checkpoint — press Continue (or Enter) when you're ready for the next one. Choose how many parts it has (4 to 12 minutes) in Settings. Doing one loop a day is the simplest way to improve steadily.

Aa Capitals, punctuation and numbers

The three small switches above the text let you choose what appears in your practice: capital letters, punctuation marks, and numbers. Turn them on when you feel ready — real-world typing uses all three.

📈 Dashboard

Your progress at a glance: how much you have practiced, your average and best speeds, charts of speed and accuracy over time, and an activity calendar that shows the days you practiced.

♛ Milestones

Speed badges you earn as you get faster. A badge is only awarded when you prove the speed three times, on different texts, with high accuracy — so it means you can really type that fast, not that you got lucky once. Once earned, a badge is yours forever.

🔍 Weak Spots

Shows exactly where your typing time is being lost — which key combinations make you hesitate and which keys cause errors. The app needs about 30 tests before it knows your fingers well. Each weak pattern has a Drill button that launches a 90-second sprint just for it. When you master a pattern, it moves to the "Recently mastered" list.

⌨ Shortcuts

A learn-and-review system for keyboard shortcuts, so you can run your laptop mostly from the keyboard. Start with the Core 20% — the small set that does most of the work — and press + Learn on one or two. They appear in Review; try each one, then say Got it (it comes back later: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 days) or Fuzzy (back tomorrow). When one feels automatic, Retire ★ it — it moves to Mastered and the to-learn list shrinks. Windows and macOS are tracked separately. The Terminal sub-tab teaches Linux shell commands the same way — Essentials first, then More — and each review card has a box where you type the command back to check yourself. The Tools sub-tab does the same per application: pick VS Code or Claude Code and its shortcuts show. Terminal and Tools keep one shared list on both platforms — switching to macOS just swaps Ctrl for Cmd on screen.

💡 Tips

Accuracy first — speed follows on its own. Short daily practice beats long rare sessions. And don't worry about the weak patterns: the app finds them and trains them for you automatically.

About

TypeTrainer 3 measures where your typing time is actually lost — hesitant transitions, slow chunks and error-prone keys — then weaves the costliest ones into practice text and targeted drills. New in 3: Verified Speed Badges. A milestone is earned only with repeated evidence — three qualifying tests (98%+ accuracy at the badge speed) on different passages across at least two practice sessions — and once earned it is permanent. Two tracks run in parallel: sustained badges need 30-second tests, burst badges 15-second ones, each with its own goal speed. Everything runs offline; your data never leaves this computer.
